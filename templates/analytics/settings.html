Email tracking

Click and open tracking for email. Both are off until you turn them on. Neither affects the click counters on flow buttons, which are always recorded — a button is a link somebody added on purpose.

{% csrf_token %}

Every link in an email you send is replaced with one that records the click and forwards to the original address. Recipients see the forwarding link in their status bar rather than the destination.

A one-pixel image is added to each email and marks the message as read when it loads. Many mail clients block or pre-fetch images, so this is an indication rather than a count.
