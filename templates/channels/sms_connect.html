Connect a Twilio number

BrightBean Chat sends SMS through your own Twilio account, so the messages, the number and the bill are all yours. The Auth Token is the whole credential: anyone holding it can send as your number and read your messages, so it is stored encrypted and never shown again.

Before you paste

  1. Open the Twilio console and copy the Account SID and Auth Token from the dashboard.
  2. Copy either a phone number you own, in E.164 form (+15551234567), or a Messaging Service SID (MG…) if you send through one.
  3. US traffic also needs A2P 10DLC registration, which happens in Twilio and not here. There is a checklist on the SMS settings page.

What happens next

{% comment %} No copy field here: the webhook URL is per connection and does not exist until this form succeeds. It lives on the connection's own page, which is where somebody pasting it into Twilio is already standing. {% endcomment %}

We check the credentials and confirm the sender belongs to the account, then store the connection. Twilio has no way for us to configure your number remotely, so the last step is yours: open the number (or the messaging service) in the Twilio console and paste the webhook URL shown on the connection's page into A message comes in, as a POST.

That URL has to match exactly, trailing slash included: Twilio signs each delivery with an HMAC computed over the URL itself, so a URL that differs by one character fails verification. It also has to be reachable from the public internet over HTTPS. Full setup notes are in docs/channels/sms.md.
