Connect a Telegram bot

Telegram bots are created by talking to BotFather. The token it gives you is the whole credential: anyone holding it can read and send as your bot, so it is stored encrypted and never shown again.

Before you paste

  1. Open @BotFather in Telegram and send /newbot.
  2. Choose a display name and a username ending in bot.
  3. Copy the token it replies with. It looks like 123456789:AA….

What happens next

We call getMe to check the token and learn your bot's username, then point Telegram at this deployment with setWebhook and a secret only the two of us know.
