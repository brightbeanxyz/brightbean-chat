Email is outbound only. BrightBean Chat sends through your own provider — your SMTP server, Resend, or Amazon SES — so the sending reputation, the domain and the bill stay yours.

Before you start

Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the domain you are going to send from. All three, not one: without them a meaningful share of what you send goes to spam, and Gmail and Yahoo reject bulk mail from domains that lack them outright. Your provider publishes the exact records. The full checklist is in docs/channels/email.md.

What happens next

We check the credentials with your provider before saving anything. Once the channel exists, its page shows the webhook URL to paste into Resend or to subscribe an SNS topic to, and a button that sends a test email to your own address.

Every email BrightBean Chat sends carries a List-Unsubscribe header and an unsubscribe link in the footer. That is not configurable: it is what keeps the channel compliant, and it is what the unsubscribe and suppression handling is built on.
