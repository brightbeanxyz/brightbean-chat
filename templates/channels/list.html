Its neighbours under layouts/workspace_settings.html are still plain HTML, so this page is now ahead of them rather than behind. That is deliberate — it is the one with a first-run audience — and the idiom used here is the app's existing one (the rounded-xl/divide-y card from flows/_list_rows.html, .an-table, .status-pill, .pi-chip), so bringing a sibling up to match is a copy job rather than a design decision. Utilities here only ever set properties the design-system classes do not, per ground rule 9 in docs/agent-prompts/layer-2.md: those classes are unlayered and beat @layer utilities, so `btn-outline w-auto` would silently do nothing. {% endcomment %} {% block content %}
