The wording of the replies carriers require you to send, and a couple of hints for the composer. The replies themselves are not optional and cannot be switched off here — leaving a box empty uses the default wording rather than sending nothing.

Keywords

These are handled before anything else and cannot be intercepted by a flow. Matching is case-insensitive on the whole message, so "stop" unsubscribes and "stop by tomorrow" does not.

Cost hint


Used only to estimate what a broadcast will cost. BrightBean Chat never meters or bills anything — your Twilio invoice is the real number, and prices differ per destination.

A2P 10DLC checklist

US carriers require application-to-person traffic on a 10-digit long code to be registered. All of it happens in the Twilio console; nothing on this page performs or verifies registration, and these boxes are your own record that it is done.
