{% load common_extras %}{% comment %} The refreshable half of the contact list: the table, the empty state and the pager. Everything else on the page — the toolbar, the filter builder, the bulk bar — stays put across a swap. It re-fetches itself on `contactsChanged`, which every mutation's toast carries, the same way `_tag_rows.html` answers `tagsChanged`. Fetching the whole page and picking one div out of it with hx-select also works and costs a full shell render — sidebar, workspace switcher, every context-processor query — per keystroke-sized edit. Everything rendered here is attacker-controlled: names, emails and platform usernames all arrive from strangers (SECURITY-BASELINE §2). Django autoescapes, nothing is marked safe, and no value from a contact reaches an href or a src. {% endcomment %}
