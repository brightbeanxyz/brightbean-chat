BrightBean Chat uses the Instagram API with Instagram Login, so the account you connect must be a professional account — Business or Creator. You will be sent to Instagram to authorise it, and we never see your password.

Before you start

  1. Create a Meta app with the Instagram product added.
  2. Add this deployment's callback URL to the app's Business login settings, exactly as written:
    {{ callback_url }}
  3. Put the app's Instagram app ID and secret into Settings → Credentials, or set PLATFORM_INSTAGRAM_CLIENT_ID and PLATFORM_INSTAGRAM_CLIENT_SECRET on the deployment.
  4. In the account's Instagram settings, allow access to messages.

We ask for these permissions: {% for scope in scopes %}{{ scope }}{% if not forloop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}. Reading and answering comments needs the last one, so it is requested up front rather than when a comment trigger is first used.

What happens next

Instagram sends you back here with a one-time code. We exchange it for a long-lived token, store it encrypted, and refresh it automatically before it expires — so you should not have to reconnect. If a token is ever revoked, the channel is marked Needs reconnection and you get a notification.

One more step is not automatable: subscribe your Meta app to this account's webhook fields (messages, messaging_postbacks, comments, mentions, message_deletions). Serving accounts other than your own also needs Advanced Access, App Review and Business Verification from Meta. Both are written up in docs/channels/instagram.md.
